- Blurry document images
- Strong glare
- Incorrect personal information
- Name mismatch
- Camera permissions disabled
- Poor lighting during facial verification
- Expired documents
- Additional regional requirements

Try the following:

1. Check that all entered information matches the document.
2. Use clear, original and unedited document images.
3. Enable camera permissions.
4. Complete facial verification in good lighting.
5. Update the Binance App.
6. Follow the specific error message shown in your account.

If Binance asks for additional proof such as address verification, follow the instructions displayed for your own account.

Do not try to bypass KYC by changing information or using another person's identity documents.
